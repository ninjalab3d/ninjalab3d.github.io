3D printing from your STL, 3MF or OBJ file

Have a ready 3D model? Send it and I will print it in the material and color you choose. Before printing I check the file and warn you if there is a problem.

Send an inquiryCalculate the price

Which files I accept

STL, 3MF, OBJ and STEP. I also accept a link to a model from Thingiverse, Printables or another site, as long as the author's license allows the use.

How to prepare the file

  • The model should be closed, with no holes in the mesh.
  • STL or 3MF format, dimensions in millimeters.
  • At the right scale: check the dimensions before sending.
  • Not sure? Send it and I will check it before printing.

Material, color and lead time

You choose the material by use: PLA for decorations, PETG for functional parts, TPU for flexible parts. See the materials comparison. The lead time depends on the size and workload and is agreed in advance.

No file?

Send photos, dimensions or a sample. Often the part can be made from a sample. See 3D printed spare parts.

Frequently asked questions

What file should I send?

STL, 3MF, OBJ or STEP, with dimensions in millimeters. A link to the model works too.

Can I send you a model I found on the internet?

Yes, send a link from Thingiverse, Printables or another site. The author's license must allow the use.

How do I check whether an STL file is ready for printing?

The model must be closed, with no holes in the mesh and at the right scale. If you are not sure, send it and I will check it.

Can I order printing only, from a ready file?

Yes. That is the most common kind of order.

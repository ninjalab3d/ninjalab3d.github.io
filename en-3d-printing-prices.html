How Much Does 3D Printing Cost? 3D Printing Prices

The price of custom 3D printing is set by the material, the print time and the complexity of the model. Here is how it is calculated, and the calculator gives you a rough estimate in a minute.

Send an inquiryCalculate the price

What affects the price

  • Part weight (grams): more material means a higher price.
  • Print time (hours): the longer the printer runs, the more it costs.
  • Material: PLA is the most affordable, PETG is slightly more expensive, and TPU (flexible) is the most expensive.
  • Number of colors: multicolor printing in up to 4 colors takes more material and time.
  • Supports and finishing: complex models with many supports, sanding and painting add to the cost.
  • Quantity: with several pieces the price per piece goes down.

How to get a quick estimate

Open the model in your slicer and read the weight and print time. Enter them in the 3D printing price calculator, choose the material, colors and quantity, and you will see an approximate price. Don't know the weight or time? Send the model and I will work them out.

Minimum order and delivery

There is a minimum order price, so very small parts are best combined. Delivery is not included in the price and is sent by courier anywhere in the country.

Get an exact price

The calculator gives a rough range. I give the exact price after reviewing the model. Send an inquiry with a file, or with photos and dimensions.

Frequently asked questions

How much does custom 3D printing cost?

Is there a minimum order value?

Yes, there is a minimum order price. Small parts can be combined into one order.

Why is multicolor printing more expensive?

It needs extra material and more time for color changes. The calculator takes this into account.

Is delivery included?

No. Delivery is by courier to anywhere in the country.

3D printing of prototypes and mockups

3D printing is a fast, cheap way to check shape, size and fit before series production. Enclosures, holders and mounting parts are printed in hours, not weeks.

Send an inquiryCalculate the price

What it is good for

  • Enclosures for electronics projects.
  • Holders and mounting parts.
  • Mockups to check shape and size.
  • Single pieces and small batches.

How it works

You send a file (STL, 3MF, OBJ, STEP) or a drawing with dimensions. I check the model, give a price and lead time, and print. If needed we do a second iteration with fixes. For parts that assemble together, a small clearance is built in.

Material for a prototype

PLA is the fastest and cheapest choice for shape and size. If the prototype will be under load or in warmth, we choose PETG. Materials.

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to print a prototype?

From a few hours for small parts to several days for large ones. The lead time is agreed in advance.

What is the print accuracy?

With FDM printing the typical accuracy is about 0.2 mm.

Can I order just one prototype?

Yes, I accept single pieces too. There is a minimum order price.

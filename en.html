    What affects the price of 3D printing

    The price of 3D printing is determined mainly by four things: how much material goes into the part (weight in grams), how many hours the printer runs, which filament is chosen and how many colors are needed. Multicolor printing and complex models with many supports cost more, because they need more material and time.

    Extra costs are sanding and painting. With several pieces the price per piece goes down. The quickest way to get an idea is the calculator above: you only need the weight and time that the slicer shows for your STL file.     Which material should I choose for 3D printing?

    PLA or PETG, TPU: different filaments have different properties. Here is a short guide, and the full comparison is here.

    PLA: for decorations, models and figurines

    The most commonly used material. It prints easily, gives good detail and comes in a wide range of colors. It softens at around 55-60 degrees, so it is not for places in direct sun or in a car in summer.

    PETG: for functional parts

    Stronger and more resistant to moisture and chemicals than PLA, with slight flexibility. It handles about 70-80 degrees and suits parts for the home, kitchen and outdoor use.

    TPU: flexible parts

    A rubber-like material for seals, protectors, cases and shock absorbers.

    Frequently asked questions about 3D printing

    How much does custom 3D printing cost?

    The price depends on the part weight, print time, material, number of colors and finishing. The calculator gives a rough range, and the exact price is given after I review the model. There is a minimum order price.

    What files do you accept (STL, 3MF, OBJ)?

    STL, 3MF, OBJ and STEP, as well as a link from Thingiverse or Printables. If you don't have a file, send photos and dimensions.

    Can I order if I don't have a 3D model, only a sample or a photo?

    Yes, it is often possible from a sample or dimensions. Send photos from several sides and the main dimensions, and if you have the broken part, that helps even more.

    Do you accept orders for a single piece?

    Yes, I accept single pieces too. There is a minimum order price, and with several pieces the price per piece goes down.

    PLA or PETG: which material should I choose?

    PLA suits decorations, models and gifts, while PETG is for parts that carry load, meet moisture or sit in warmth up to about 70 degrees. If you are not sure, describe where the part will be used and I will recommend a material.

    Can a printed part be used outdoors or in a car in summer?

    PLA softens at around 55-60 degrees and is not suitable for direct sun or a car in summer. For such places PETG is used, which handles about 70-80 degrees.

    How many colors can a 3D printed model have?

    Up to 4 colors in one part. The colors are part of the material itself, so they do not peel off like paint.

    How long does 3D printing take?

    From a few hours for small parts to several days for large ones or ones made in several pieces. The lead time depends on the size and current workload and is agreed in advance.

    Do you deliver to Sofia, Plovdiv and other cities?

    Yes, orders are sent by courier to anywhere in the country, including Sofia and Plovdiv.

    Can you print a model I found on the internet?

    Yes, send a link from Thingiverse, Printables or another site. I check the model before printing, and the author's license must allow the use.

    What is the print accuracy?

    With FDM printing the typical accuracy is about 0.2 mm. For parts that fit together, a small clearance is built in so they fit.

    How do I prepare a file for 3D printing?

    It is best if the model is closed, with no holes in the mesh, in STL or 3MF format and with dimensions in millimeters. If you are not sure, send it and I will check it.

    Are printed parts strong?

    Strength depends on the material, infill density and layer direction. For parts under load I choose PETG and more walls.
